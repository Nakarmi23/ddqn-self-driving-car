# Reward-design literature for the DDQN racing comparison

Compiled 2026-11-26 from primary sources (arXiv full text, publisher/proceedings pages, author PDFs).
Conventions used throughout:

- **Paper says** = taken from the primary source, with section/equation.
- **Inference (ours)** = our reading of how it applies to this codebase. Never attributed to the paper.
- **unverified** = could not be checked against a primary source; left in place deliberately.
- Formulas are transcribed from the paper's notation. Where the extracted text was garbled, that is stated and the formula is not reproduced.
- Relevance is scored against *this* setup: single car, wall-bounded loop track, 17-D ray/kinematic observation, 9 discrete actions, value-based (DDQN) learner. **H / M / L**.
- Peer-reviewed venue is given where verified; arXiv-only work is flagged **[arXiv-only]**.

**Direct D1-vs-D2 ablation in racing/navigation RL: none found** (queries in §4). Closest evidence:

*Inference (ours), D1 vs D2:* a checkpoint bonus `+b` per checkpoint equals PBRS with `Φ(s) = b·k(s)` only if `k` is the **cumulative** checkpoint count, not the index mod 38, and if the γ factor is applied (`γΦ(s')−Φ(s)`). Per V9, Φ must also be reset to 0 at crash and stall terminals. Otherwise the bonus changes the optimal policy, and Knox check (1) "unsafe reward shaping" applies. This is worth stating explicitly in the thesis because it makes D1 vs D2 a test of *shaping form*, not of objective.

**Starting values to borrow:** `c_w = 5×10⁻⁴` with progress in metres and speed in m/s (Fuchs Table II). Fuchs uses γ = 0.98–0.982 against our 0.99.

**Recommended thesis claim (wording, ours):** "To our knowledge, no prior work compares additive and multiplicative per-step reward aggregation for a value-based (DQN-family) agent in driving or racing under a matched tuning budget. Multiplicative composition has been proposed for actor-critic control [Mysore 2021] and used in driving planners trained with PPO [CaRL 2025], but its effect has not been isolated." Avoid "first to propose multiplicative rewards".

*Inference (ours), DDQN-specific risks for D5:* (i) if progress can be negative (reversing), `progress × gate` lets a low gate *reduce the penalty* for going backwards, so clip progress at 0 or gate only the positive part. (ii) A hard 0 gate flattens Q-differences in risky states, which is the fixed-point issue that Mysore's +ε addresses. (iii) Gates multiply the progress scale, so the effective reward range differs from D4's and the Huber transition point moves (see Protocol).
